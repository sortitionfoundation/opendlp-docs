title: Cookies
description: What cookies are set by OpenDLP and why
---

Cookies are small files saved on your device when you visit a website. OpenDLP uses
cookies only to make the service work — to keep you signed in, to keep forms secure, and
to remember your language choice.

**We do not use cookies for advertising, and we never will. We do not use Google Analytics
or any other tracking or analytics that identifies you or your device.**

Because every cookie we set is essential to providing the service you asked for, we do not
need to ask for your consent, and there is no cookie banner. This page tells you what we
store and why, which the law requires us to do.

## Cookies we use

| Name             | Purpose                                                                                                                                          | Expires                                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `session`        | Keeps forms secure, keeps you signed in, shows one-off messages (such as "your details were saved"), and remembers the language you chose.       | When you close your browser, or after 7 days, whichever is sooner. |
| `remember_token` | Set **only if you tick "Keep me signed in"** when you sign in. Keeps you signed in on this device so you do not have to sign in again each time. | After 7 days.                                                      |

Both cookies are set by OpenDLP itself. Neither is shared with anyone else, and neither can
be read by another website.

## Choosing to stay signed in

The `remember_token` cookie is the one cookie that is not strictly required. We set it only
when you tick the **"Keep me signed in"** box when signing in. That box is unticked by
default, so nothing is stored unless you choose it. If you leave it unticked, you stay
signed in only until you close your browser.

## Your language choice

If you choose a language, we remember it so you do not have to choose again on every page.
This preference is stored inside the `session` cookie above. You can change it at any time
by choosing a different language.

## Cookies from other companies

OpenDLP does not set any third-party cookies, and does not embed advertising, social media
buttons, or analytics services that would set them.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. If you block the `session`
cookie, some parts of OpenDLP — signing in, and submitting forms — will not work, because
that cookie is what keeps those actions secure.

Guidance for common browsers:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
