title: Democratic Lottery timeline
description: How long does it take to plan and organise a democratic lottery?

---

Running the selection for a democratic lottery is nearly instantaneous - once everything is set up, you press a button and within a few minutes, the software will give you the details of the people selected, along with the names of those who have not been selected.

**Before you start**

- Are there any holidays happening? That could delay things.

- How long will it take to print invites? We've put in two weeks, but some printers are faster, some slower.

- How long will posting invites take? The time of year, how good your postal service is, how remote your area will all affect when invites land on doorsteps.

**The timeline**

**1. Designing the invite**  - you want an invitation that is easy to read, that encourages people to register, and gives all the essential information. We allow **four weeks** for this stage.

**2. Printing and posting** - from the day you send the materials to the printer, we allow two weeks before invites are posted. Then another week for them to land on doorsteps. **Three weeks**  in total.

**3. Selection and confirmation** - **one week** for this stage.

**4. Preparing materials, technology etc** - facilitators and commissioning organisations need to ensure that they have translation, that participants have background materials, that any other needs are met. This stage usually needs **three weeks.**

While the timeline above adds up to 11 weeks, we usually try to allow 12 weeks from the start of recruitment to the first meeting - the buffer time helps to relieve some pressure!
